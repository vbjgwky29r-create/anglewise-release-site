Privacy Policy

Your photos, notes and angle values stay on your device. Starting with version 1.1.4, you may choose to share limited usage information to help improve the app.

Photos, measurements and permissions

Taking or importing photos, adjusting angle lines, editing notes, saving drafts and creating annotated images happen on your device. The app does not require an account or cloud processing for these features. Level and inclinometer readings and calibration are processed on your device; saved measurement records are local. If you choose to share a record, the system share sheet sends the content to the destination you select.

Camera access is used when you choose to take a photo. Photo library add access is used when you choose to save an annotated image. Photos, notes and angle values are not uploaded to the usage receiver.

Optional usage sharing in 1.1.4 and later

Sharing is off until you select “Allow sharing.” Choosing not to share does not change features, prices or Pro access. You can change your choice at any time from “Privacy & improvements” on the home screen.

If you allow sharing, the app sends stages and outcomes of measurement adjustments, exports, Pro and paywall visits, product loading, purchases and restores. Reports include the app version, UTC date and temporary random session and operation identifiers. Session identifiers change whenever the app starts and are not account, installation or device identifiers. Versions with level and inclinometer tools also report the selected tool mode and the stages and outcomes of opening a tool, holding a reading, calibration, saving or viewing a local record, and opening the share sheet. Opening the share sheet does not tell us whether or to whom you shared.

Usage information is used only to understand and improve measurement, export and purchase experiences. We do not link it to your identity, other apps, advertising profiles or outside datasets.

Purchases and information not collected

Apple handles the optional one-time Pro purchase. The app may report a purchase or restore outcome, such as success, cancellation, pending or failure, if you allow sharing.

Usage reports do not contain photos, notes, angle values, raw sensor readings, calibration values, saved record contents, names, accounts, stable device identifiers, location, prices, payment details, receipts or Apple transaction numbers. There are no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

Hosting, retention and withdrawal

Wang Jiachen maintains the first-party usage receiver on Tencent Cloud infrastructure in Nanjing, mainland China. Raw events are retained for at most 30 days and periodically deleted. The bounded on-device unsent queue expires within seven days and is excluded from device backups.

Network delivery necessarily uses an IP address. The usage service does not store it in request logs or the analytics database. Brief request throttling uses an in-memory temporary digest that expires within one minute. We do not use it to identify users or determine their location.

Choosing “Do not share” stops future collection and clears unsent events. A request already in flight may have reached the receiver; received events expire under the retention period above. Because reports are not linked to an account or device identity, individual records generally cannot be located by your identity.

Earlier versions and contact

Versions 1.1.3 and earlier do not include this optional usage sharing. Their photos and notes remain on device and they do not send these new usage events. Installing an update does not enable sharing without your choice.

For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact 173639767@qq.com.

照片与测量留在本机

拍照或导入、调节角度线、编辑备注、保存草稿及生成标注图片均在设备上处理，不需要注册账号或依赖云端处理。水平仪和倾角仪的读数与校准在设备上处理，测量记录保存在本机。只有你主动分享记录时，系统分享面板才会将内容发送至你选择的目的地。相机权限用于你主动拍照，相册写入权限用于你主动保存图片。照片、备注和角度数值不会上传至统计接收服务。

1.1.4 及以后版本的可选使用统计

默认不共享，只有你主动选择“允许共享”后才运行。选择“不共享”不会影响功能、价格或 Pro 权益；你可以在首页“隐私与改进”随时更改选择。

允许后发送的内容包括：调线、导出、打开 Pro 和付费页、商品加载、购买或恢复等操作的阶段与结果，以及 App 版本、UTC 日期、临时随机会话和操作关联编号。会话编号在每次启动时更换，不是账号、安装或设备标识。包含水平仪和倾角仪的版本还会报告所选工具模式，以及打开工具、锁定读数、校准、保存或查看本机记录、打开分享面板等操作的阶段与结果。打开分享面板不表示已完成分享，也不会告诉我们分享给了谁。

这些信息仅用于理解并改善测量、导出与购买体验，不关联你的身份、其他 App、广告档案或外部身份数据。

购买及不上传的内容

可选的一次性 Pro 购买由 Apple 处理。允许共享后，App 可能报告购买或恢复的成功、取消、待处理、失败等结果。

统计不包含照片、备注、角度数值、原始传感器读数、校准数值、保存记录的内容、姓名、账号、稳定设备标识、位置、价格、付款资料、收据或 Apple 交易编号。不接入广告或第三方统计 SDK，不进行跨 App 追踪。

存储、保留与关闭

统计服务由 Wang Jiachen 维护，部署在中国大陆南京的腾讯云基础设施上。原始事件最多保留 30 天并定期清理。本机待发送队列有数量上限、最多保留 7 天，且不参与设备备份。

网络传输必然使用 IP 地址。统计接收服务不将其写入请求日志或统计数据库；短期防滥用仅使用一分钟内有效的临时内存摘要，不用于识别用户或推断位置。

选择“不共享”后停止后续采集并清除尚未发送的数据；已经开始传输的请求可能已经到达服务器，已收到的事件按上述期限清理。由于统计未绑定账号或设备身份，通常无法根据你的身份查找单条记录。

旧版本与联系
