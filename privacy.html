AngleWise / 角知道

Privacy Policy

AngleWise is designed as a local photo measurement tool. The current version does not require account creation or cloud processing for core features.

Effective date: 2026-03-24
Contact: 173639767@qq.com

What the app does

AngleWise lets you take a photo, place two reference lines, calculate the angle between them, add a note, and save the annotated image to your photo library.

Data collection

AngleWise does not collect personal data for its core functionality. Photos and notes are processed locally on your device.

  • Camera access is used only to capture photos when you choose to take one.
  • Photo library add access is used only when you choose to save an annotated image.
  • The app does not create an account for you.
  • The app does not upload your photos or notes to a backend service in the current version.

Purchases

AngleWise offers a 3-day app-managed free trial and a one-time lifetime unlock. Purchase transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store. AngleWise does not store your payment card information.

Third parties

The current version does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking.
